♜ Chess Tips

♟

Control the Center

Try to control the four central squares. This gives your pieces more freedom to move.

♘

Develop Your Pieces

Bring your knights and bishops into the game early. Avoid moving the same piece repeatedly without a reason.

♔

Protect Your King

Castle when appropriate to help keep your king safe.

♕

Think Before Moving

Check whether your opponent can capture your piece before making a move.

♞ Learn Chess Tactics

⚔️

Fork

One piece attacks two or more enemy pieces at the same time.

📌

Pin

An enemy piece cannot move easily because doing so would expose a more valuable piece behind it.

🎯

Skewer

Attack a valuable piece so that it moves away and exposes another piece behind it.

👑

Checkmate

Trap the enemy king so it is in check and has no legal way to escape.
